# D-0007 — The bulk pass

Supersedes D-0004's seam numbers only; the rest of D-0004 (the vocabulary, the budget, no cube buried, the
choices made building the seven) stands.

**The ask.** A friend, after 0.1.0 (pack 1.80.0): "arms look really chunky and blocky when equipped in first
person and third person. Could stand to be more slimmed down and fitted so its not so obstructive or clips into
player body as much. Should do a bulkyness pass over all pieces." nfx's §18 had left the door open ("I'd rather
push it and pull back"). Rusty, on the proposal below: "Give it a shot, if its still not good I'll let you know."

**What 0.1.0 measured.** The sleeves themselves were no bulkier than vanilla's chestplate (0.9 to 1.0 off the arm;
vanilla's is 1.0). The bulk was what stood on them: bracers 1.15 to 1.65 out; every bracer 1.1 to 1.3 inside the
arm's inner face, where the arm swings past the torso; left shoulders 1.6 to 2.2 out, 1.6 to 1.9 before and
behind, 1.9 to 2.85 above the shoulder, into the hood; torsos 3.2 to 4.2 deep in front and up to 4.85 behind; and
torso cubes out at 5.0 to 6.4 to the side, where the arms hang (Hazmat's bedroll, Magpie's tool pouch, Duster's
rag strips). In first person the bracer at the wrist, at the end of the arm you look along, stood as a block in the
lower right of the view (the booth's `booth-first-person`).

**Decision.** One envelope, in `devtools/art/kitlib/vocab.py`, held for every kit by the art tests
(`test_arms_…`, `test_the_body_…`, `test_helmets_legs_and_boots_keep_to_the_envelope`, and a probe that the
measures bite). How far a cube may stand off the body part it rides on, measured where its corners stand:

| Zone | 0.1.0 (over the seven) | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Sleeve | 0.9 to 1.0 | 0.5 (`SLEEVE`) |
| Below the shoulder: out, before, behind | 1.15 to 1.65 | 1.0 (`ARM_OUT`) |
| An arm's side toward the body | 1.1 to 1.3 | 0.6 (`ARM_INNER`) |
| Left shoulder: out / before and behind / above | 1.6 to 2.2 / 1.6 to 1.9 / 1.9 to 2.85 | 1.5 / 1.2 / 0.75 |
| Right shoulder: out / before and behind / above | 1.0 to 1.5 / 1.0 to 1.3 / 0.5 to 1.8 | 1.0 / 1.0 / 0.5 |
| Torso, before and behind | 3.2 to 4.2, to 4.85 | 3.0 |
| Torso to the side, between y 11.5 and 22 | to 5.0 to 6.4 | 4.9 (5.0 elsewhere) |
| Helmet before the face / to the side | 1.6 to 4.0 / to 3.65 | 2.75 / 2.5 |
| Leggings on a leg, every side | 0.5 to 1.45 | 1.0 |
| Boots to the side / before and behind | 1.0 to 1.25 / 2.0 to 2.25 | 1.0 / 1.5 |

An arm cube wholly above y 20 is the shoulder's; any other is held to the sleeve's limits.

The seams move in with the torso's undersuit (0.9 to 0.6): a chest piece's inner layer at 2.6, the leggings' room
above the hem 4.45 side to side and 2.55 before and behind. Boot shafts come from 0.95 to 0.75 (boots stand outside
2.75), so a cuff at the 1.0 limit still steps off its boot.

**First person** draws the kit's sleeve alone: `KitMesh.sleeve(arm)` is the arm's cubes that keep within `SLEEVE`
(`KitMesh.SLEEVE`, 0.5) of the arm on every side but the hand's end, judged where their corners stand, culled among
themselves so a sleeve top a shoulder covered is drawn. Your arm wears the kit's sleeve; nothing strapped on it
(bracers, gloves, tape, the shoulder) stands in the view down it. nfx's §13 asks for the same geometry as third
person; this keeps its sleeve and drops what blocked the view. Built first was the shoulder left out (every arm cube
wholly above y 20): the booth's first-person photo showed it changed nothing, since the shoulder is never in that
view and the block was the bracer.

**Choices the limits forced:**

- Hazmat's bedroll no longer runs past the right shoulder: past it is where the right arm swings. It lies across
  the shoulder blades, inside 4.8.
- Magpie's top busbar lies flat. Turned, its outer end dipped below the sleeve's top, which showed through it as a
  dark box; the lower busbar keeps the bend. The insulator is a squat ceramic cap on top.
- A band on an arm stands thinner on the body's side (`band(…, inner=)`), so a bracer keeps its step outside and
  stays clear of the torso. A pouch on a leg keeps a thinner flap (`pouch(…, flap=)`).
- Duster's coat panels stop at 4.8, so its rag strips at 4.9 never share a plane with them.
- Every kit's sheets shrank: the block atlas carries 9.57 Mpx of kit sprites, from 10.67.

**Alternatives rejected.** A separate first-person model (§13 rules it out, and two sets drift apart). Scaling each
kit toward the body by a factor: it thins the cubes and the steps between layers, not the stacking that made the
bulk. Dropping the heavy left shoulder (§5's asymmetry stays, compressed).
