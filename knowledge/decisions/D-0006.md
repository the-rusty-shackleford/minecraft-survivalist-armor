# D-0006 — The kits in EMI

**The ask.** The plan: an EMI plugin, compile-only, as Warehouse Manager's. EMI shows a smithing
recipe only when it is one of the game's own (`SmithingTransformRecipe`, `SmithingTrimRecipe`), and
a `CustomRecipe` not at all, so the pack's recipe viewer would show a kit's crafting and copying but
never what the kit is for, nor how to take one off.

**Decision.** `integration/emi/SurvivalistEmiPlugin`, found by EMI's entrypoint scan, nothing else
referencing it:

- one smithing recipe per kit, laid out as the table is: kit, piece, scrap, result. The piece and
  the kitted piece turn together through every piece the kit goes on, as EMI's own trim recipes
  turn: generated slots on one seed. Pieces the server refuses are left out.
- one crafting recipe for the removal: a kitted piece and shears give the piece as it was, the
  examples turning through kits and pieces.

The booths run EMI (`gametestRuntimeOnly`), and the booth checks that every kit's recipe and the
removal are in EMI's recipe manager, then photographs a kit's uses.
