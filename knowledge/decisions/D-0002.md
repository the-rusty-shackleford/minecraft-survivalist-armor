# D-0002 — A clean-room match to Pixlli

**The ask.**

- nfx (§7, §9): textures at 128× with LabPBR `_n` and `_s` maps that sit among Pixlli's under
  shaders, with surfaces "derived from the Pixlli source art rather than drawn from scratch".
- Pixlli's `LICENSE` (illystray.com): the user "may edit digital products, for personal use only"
  and "may not upload digital products to any website without the prior written permission". Our
  repos and GitHub releases are public.
- Rusty, 2026-10-09: "Eat Pixlli's lunch as close as possible without infringing on a license. Clean
  room copy?"

**Decision.** A wall between measuring Pixlli and painting our own.

- **`devtools/art/measure/`** reads Rusty's own copy of the pack and writes `pixlli-style.json`.
  - Per material class (stone, oak planks, iron, gold, diamond and netherite blocks, leather, wool,
    chain, copper) it keeps scalars and palettes of at most eight colours: value percentiles, hue
    and saturation spread, edge-to-panel contrast, grain scale, and the ranges of each LabPBR
    channel.
  - No image, crop, trace or downscale of Pixlli's art crosses.
- **`devtools/art/paint/`** never imports `measure/` and never opens a Pixlli file. It paints our
  own procedural materials over the kits' geometry.
  - A test runs it with no Pixlli present and fails on anything image-sized in the style file.
- **Calibration** is numeric against the style file first, then by eye in game beside Pixlli's stone
  and oak planks, daylight and cave, under Complementary.

**Alternatives rejected.**

- *Deriving surfaces from Pixlli's art.* Its licence forbids uploading derivatives, and our
  releases are public.
- *A private, Pixlli-derived patch shipped only inside the pack.* Rusty chose the clean room.
- *Original art, unmeasured.* It would read as a different pack beside Pixlli's blocks.

**Verification.** In Phase 1: the wall test, the channel and value tests, and the Roadplate
checkpoint photographed beside stone and oak planks under shaders.
