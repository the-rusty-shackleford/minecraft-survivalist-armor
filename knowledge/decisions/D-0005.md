# D-0005 — Modded armour

**The ask.** nfx (§12): accept anything in the armour slot tags; a palette table keyed by armour
material with a neutral default; palette entries for nfx's own galestone and voidsteel; a blacklist
for armour whose geometry fights the kits.

**What the pack carries (swept 2026-10-09, Rusty's instance's 131 jars).** Armour in the slot tags
comes from Myths of the Sea, Block Factory's Bosses, Create (cardboard, the diving gear, the
backtanks), Mowzie's Mobs (the geomancer set, the masks, the wrought helmet), galestone and
voidsteel. A kit replaces whatever draws inside the armour layer, GeckoLib armour included (the
hook cancels `renderArmorPiece` before NeoForge's armour-model hook). What draws outside it would
show through a kit: only Create's `BacktankArmorLayer`, which draws the tank on the back of any
entity whose chest slot holds a backtank. Mowzie's `GeckoArmorLayer` dresses only its transformed
player model.

**Decision.**

- `galestone:galestone` takes a pale, wind-cut stone palette (hue 203, as its own textures);
  `voidsteel:voidsteel` a dark blue-violet steel whose albedo is its metal. Both are greyed to the
  cap. Every other material falls to `neutral`.
- `create:copper_backtank` and `create:netherite_backtank` are in the server's `blacklist` by
  default, with the reason in the config's comment.

**Not done.** A whole-pack look at every modded piece kitted on a stand: the sweep above reads each
mod's armour classes and layers instead. Myths of the Sea's and Block Factory's pieces have not
been seen kitted.
