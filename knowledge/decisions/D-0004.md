# D-0004 — Fixed interfaces between pieces, tested

> Its seam numbers were moved in by D-0007 (2026-10-10, the bulk pass); the reasoning here stands.

**The ask.** nfx (§8): pieces of different kits mixed on one body meet without clipping; each kit
carries the shared vocabulary (§5: a respirator or sealed mask, lenses, one or two hazard
placements, a bandolier of four to six cases from the right shoulder to the left hip, pouches, a
hose, one stencil) and nothing mirrors.

**Decision.** Every kit keeps the same envelopes at the seams (`devtools/art/kitlib/vocab.py`):

- a chest piece's body stops at y 12.5; below y 14 it either stands clear of the leggings' room or
  wraps all of it;
- leggings rise no higher than y 14, and above the hem stay within 4.75 of the body's centre side
  to side and 2.85 front and back;
- boots stop at y 6.6, where knee pads and coat skirts begin; below that, trouser legs stay within
  2.6 of the leg's centre and boots stand outside 2.9.

`devtools/art/tests` holds every kit to them, to the vocabulary, to a heavy left shoulder, to a
budget of 6 to 40 cubes a piece and 160 a kit, and to no cube buried: a cube whose best face is
less than a quarter visible was drawn and painted for nothing, or meant something it no longer
shows. The first run found Duster's boot buckle inside its boot and Blacklung's bandolier below its
hem. The tests run in `./gradlew check` (`artTests`, under uv), with the clean-room wall's and the
textures' LabPBR channel tests.

**Choices the tests forced, and others made while building the seven:**

- **Duster's big collar is the hood's drape, on the helmet.** A collar on the coat would be cut by
  every hood's corner as the wearer looks round (a hood box's corner reaches 6.7 from the neck);
  on the hood it turns with the head and tucks under any chest piece's pauldrons.
- **Chain-link is see-through.** A mesh cube hides nothing (`Materials.seeThrough`, mirrored in
  the painter's culling), and a panel drops the face toward the body, so the jacket shows through
  the holes rather than a second weave. Diamonds a block and a half across, two-pixel wire, the
  panel's edge bound: nfx's "diamond weave, not rings", readable at arm's length.
- **Hazmat's suit is free of the saturation cap**, as hazard tape is: a hazmat suit is hazard
  yellow. Quilting is diamond cells; rows of seams read as planks.
- **Each preview is drawn in the armour the kit is designed toward first** (`PALETTE`): Roadplate
  and Hazmat iron, Magpie gold, Bluecoat diamond, Blacklung netherite, Duster leather, Scrapper
  chainmail. Diamond's teal was greyed (saturation 0.18), nfx wanting gold and diamond the least
  saturated at a glance.
- **Sheets are rounded to sixteen rows, not to a power of two.** Every pixel is on the block atlas
  beside Pixlli's 128x; a power of two doubled Duster's garment. Sixteen is what four mipmap levels
  need. The seven kits' sprites came to about 10 Mpx with all ten palettes.
- **A kit template's icon is its signature piece**, drawn from the kit's own textures with its own
  maps, so the seven are told apart at a glance (Roadplate's sign, Blacklung's lit eyes, Hazmat's
  hood). The scrap slot's ghost is drawn as the game's own: a 16-pixel silhouette in one grey.

**Alternatives rejected.** Per-pair fitting (each kit checked against each other kit): 49 pairs
to keep right by hand, where one envelope per seam makes any pair fit.
