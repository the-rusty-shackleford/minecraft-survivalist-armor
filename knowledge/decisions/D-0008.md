# D-0008 — The kits' own atlas

**The ask.** Rusty's client logged its block atlas at `16384x8192x4` on pack 1.80.0, `8192x8192x4` in
every session before. 1.78.0, 1.79.0 and 1.80.0 had all landed since his last session, so the
kits were suspected, not proven. Rusty: prove it; if it is the kits, show the fixes.

**Measured, not estimated.** A copy of Rusty's Prism instance (his mods, his resource packs with
Pixlli 128x, his options), launched on the 4070 each time, the block atlas read from the log.
Only the Survivalist Armor jar changed between runs:

| Run | Kit sprites on the block atlas | Block atlas |
|---|---|---|
| His client on 1.80.0, with 0.1.0 | 10.67 Mpx | 16384x8192 |
| The same, the jar removed | none | 8192x8192 |
| 0.2.0 as gated (D-0007) | 9.57 Mpx | 16384x8192 |
| 0.2.0, its atlas list emptied | none | 8192x8192 |
| The seven garments, no palette | 3.10 Mpx | 16384x8192 |
| 0.2.0 at half resolution | 2.39 Mpx | 16384x8192 |
| Four kits' garments | 1.73 Mpx | 16384x8192 |
| Two kits' garments | 0.75 Mpx | 8192x8192 |

So the kits alone doubled it, and the pack without them has under 1.73 Mpx of the 67.1 free.

**Why not shrink the kits.**

- *Fewer palettes.* The garments alone, with no palette at all, double it.
- *Tighter sheets.* The sheets are 64 to 70% faces; the faces alone are 5.62 Mpx at 128x (2.08
  garment, 3.54 across ten palettes). Half resolution doubles it too (measured); only a quarter,
  32 to a block, would fit, a quarter of Pixlli's density beside 128x armour.

**Decision.** Rusty: "option #1 is the only real option".

- **The kits' sprites are stitched on `survivalistarmor:textures/atlas/kits.png`**, a
  `TextureAtlasHolder` as the game's paintings and mob effects are (`KitAtlas`), listed in
  `assets/survivalistarmor/atlases/kits.json`. A pack adding a kit lists its sprites there; the
  file stacks across packs.
- The worn kit, the sleeve in first person and the kitted item draw with the game's own render
  types on that atlas (`KitAtlas.WORN`, `KitAtlas.ITEM`).
- **A kitted item's particle icon is its armour item's own sprite on the block atlas**:
  `BreakingItemParticle` asks the item's model and draws on the block atlas (`TERRAIN_SHEET`).
- The holder stitches without mipmaps. Nothing is lost: the kits' render types never sampled
  mipmaps on the block atlas (`armorCutoutNoCull` and `entityCutout` set none), and this pack's
  block atlas is at mip level 0 anyway.
- Iris registers its atlas PBR loader for every `TextureAtlas` by class (iris-neoforge 1.8.14,
  `PBRTextureLoaderRegistry`), so Complementary reads the kits' `_n` and `_s` here as on the block
  atlas. Seen in the GPU booth with spike S4's placeholder (below).

**What it costs.** No art changed; the sheets keep D-0004's sixteen-row rounding, which costs a few
rows a sheet on an atlas without mipmaps. The kits' atlas stitches at 4096x4096 (64 MiB); the block
atlas, at mip level 0 in this pack, goes from 512 to 256 MiB. With Complementary, Iris keeps a normal
and a specular copy of each atlas: 1.5 GiB in all before (the block atlas's three), 0.94 GiB after
(both atlases' six).

**Still true after it.** The pack's block atlas has under 1.73 Mpx free at 8192x8192: the next 128x
content anywhere in the pack doubles it again, kits or none.

**Verification.** The atlas probe with 0.2.0 built on this, Rusty's client on the 4070:
`8192x8192x4` for the block atlas and `4096x4096x0` for the kits', no error naming the mod. The
booth (software and the 4070): every kit sprite is on the kits' atlas and none on the block atlas;
the item's particle icon is the iron chestplate's own; worn, first person and items draw on the new
render types. The art tests: no kit sprite listed on the block atlas. Under Complementary the
loud-maps placeholder still shows its studs where its plain twin is flat, as before the move
(`run/review/0.2.0-d0008/s4-maps-grazing-3x-before-after.png`).
